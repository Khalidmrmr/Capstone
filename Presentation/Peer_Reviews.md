# Peer Reviews — Fall Design Presentations

**Team:** Bull Riders — Arya Garg, Clare Hooper, Rehman Khalid, Jace Schubert

Each student reviews at least three other teams' recorded presentations (teams listed near our row), enters each review in that team's first empty Peer-Reviews column of the Senior Design Team Table ending with their email, then submits the list of reviewed projects with comments on Canvas.

Each review is a constructive suggestion or a clarifying question, from one sentence to a paragraph or more.

| 1 | Nibl |
The concept behind Nibl is engaging and clearly addresses a real need, and the core features are easy to understand at a glance. That said, the app overlaps a lot with Beli, which already lets users rank restaurants, build lists, and see where their friends eat, so it’s not yet clear what would make someone switch. Identifying one or two standout features that Beli doesn’t offer, and building the pitch around them, would make Nibl’s value much more convincing.|
