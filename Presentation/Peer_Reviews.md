# Peer Reviews — Fall Design Presentations

Each student reviews at least three other teams' recorded presentations (teams listed near our row), enters each review in that team's first empty Peer-Reviews column of the Senior Design Team Table ending with their email, then submits the list of reviewed projects with comments on Canvas.

| 1 | Voya | Voya | Building Voya as a React web app is a great technical choice for a trip planning platform, and the one-stop shop approach for travel expenses is a strong foundation. Features like temperature tracking, real-world cost comparisons, viewing other people's budgets, and sharing or remixing trip plans really elevate the concept beyond a basic itinerary builder. Since travel planning is a very saturated market, the biggest challenge will be standing out from established tools. Leaning heavily into those unique social and budgeting features, especially remixing plans and referencing real-world costs, will make Voya's value proposition much clearer and more compelling. | Yes |
